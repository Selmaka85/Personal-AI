## 🎯 How to Customize

1. **Pick Relevant Bullets**: Choose 3-5 bullet points most relevant to the job
2. **Add Quantifiers**: Include specific numbers (8+ models, 35+ tests, etc.)
3. **Use Action Verbs**: Start with "Developed", "Built", "Implemented", "Designed"
4. **Highlight Impact**: Focus on what you achieved, not just what you did
5. **Match Job Description**: Align bullet points with job requirements

## 💡 Tips

- **Be Specific**: Use concrete examples and numbers
- **Show Impact**: Focus on results and achievements
- **Use Keywords**: Include relevant technologies and skills
- **Keep It Concise**: 1-2 lines per bullet point
